# Laser Miner Rig — Excalibur side
**Status: CONCEPT. Companion to the Aetherion hardware vision doc.**
**Date: 2026-09-27**

Canonical design doc: `aetherion-oracle-arcane/hardware/aetherion-qai/product-vision/laser-miner-rig.md`

## What it is for Excalibur
The owned-hashpower answer. A photonic (laser-driven) mining rig — femtosecond laser
driver, a time-bin temporal entanglement layer anchored by actinium-225 decay
(unforgeable proof-of-when for the rig's receipts), AU13 gold-cluster
interconnects, aerogel thermal envelope — designed to hash SHA-256d at a targeted
order-of-magnitude better joules-per-terahash than commodity ASICs. The
entanglement layer is the most speculative element; no such device exists.

## Why Excalibur needs it
- Merged mining with BTC borrows security; the rig *owns* it.
- The snapshot airdrop distributes coins; the rig gives those coins a native mining
  economy to land in.
- Deep-reorg guard + checkpoints are the stopgap; native photonic hashpower is the
  endgame.

## Sequencing (from the distribution plan)
1. Merged mining first — immediate security, no hardware required.
2. Claim tooling in the Deployment Portal — snapshot coins need a UX.
3. Photonic core lab demo — one optical SHA-256d round at measured J/TH before any
   hardware promise.
4. Rig fleet — only after the demo proves the physics.

## Non-claims
No prototype exists. No performance measured. This is the direction, not the device.
